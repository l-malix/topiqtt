# Privacy Policy — TopiQTT

TopiQTT is a local desktop MQTT client and explorer.

## Data Collection & Network Communication

- **No telemetry or analytics:** TopiQTT does not collect, store, or transmit usage analytics, crash reports, or personal information to the developers or any third party.
- **No automatic update checks:** TopiQTT does not contact external update servers in the background.
- **User-initiated connections only:** TopiQTT will not transfer any information to networked systems unless specifically requested by the user (connecting to, subscribing to, or publishing to an MQTT broker explicitly configured by the user).
- **Local storage:** Connection profiles, pinned topics, dashboards, and UI preferences are stored locally on the user's machine at `%APPDATA%\TopiQTT\state.json`.
